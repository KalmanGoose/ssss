03 / Watching AI Learn Loss Curve

Reading a Loss Curve

Training Loss dropping smoothly as the model learns (BasicML)

What the Curve Tells You

  • Line goes down ↓
    The computer is actually learning.
  • Stuck & flat →
    Something is off—tweak your settings and try again.
  • Fix errors step by step by watching the curve.
When you train a model, the curve guides every adjustment Slide 4 / 8
