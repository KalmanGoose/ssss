## 貳、 中英文逐句對照（含課本完成式／文法標記）

### 【Slide 1】
* Hi Professor, hi everyone. My topic is my favorite course.
  > 教授好，大家好。我的報告主題是「我最喜歡的課」。
* Since sharing a personal project can earn bonus points, I just need a second—I'm going to open Safari right now and show you what I built!
  > 因為分享個人作品可以加分，稍微等我一秒鐘——我現在要打開 Safari 展示我做出來的專題給大家看！
* Quick question—**have you ever played** pickleball? `【Unit 2.2 現在完成式：ever】`
  > 先問大家一個小問題——你們曾經打過匹克球嗎？

### 【Slide 2】
* Actually, I built this pickleball game where you play against an Artificial Intelligence opponent—and our team **just** won first place, the Gold Award, at a coding competition last week! `【Unit 2：just】`
  > 其實我做了這款讓你跟人工智慧對手對戰的匹克球遊戲——我們團隊上週才剛在程式競賽拿下第一名（金獎）！
* You can scan the QR code right here to try it, or check out our competition poster on the slide.
  > 你們可以直接掃畫面上的 QR Code 試玩，或是看投影片上的競賽海報。
* And building the Artificial Intelligence in this game goes right back to the class I took at NTU in Fall 2025—*Introduction to Generative AI and Machine Learning* by Professor Hung-yi Lee.
  > 而這款遊戲裡的人工智慧設計，正是源自我在 2025 年秋季（114-1）修過臺大李宏毅教授的《生成式人工智慧與機器學習導論》。
* Here's what stuck with me from day one: **unless** you know how AI actually works, you're just a user. **If** you understand the ideas behind it, you can build games and tools yourself. `【Unit 4.4 條件句：unless / if】`
  > 他在第一堂課講過一句一直讓我印象深刻的話：除非你知道它背後怎麼運作，否則你只是個使用者；只要你懂原理，你也能自己做出遊戲跟工具。

### 【Slide 3】
* Before taking that class, Artificial Intelligence **had been** a total mystery to me, and I **had thought** machine learning was just scary math. `【Unit 3 過去完成式：had been / had thought】`
  > 在修那門課以前，人工智慧對我來說完全是個謎，我以前也一直以為機器學習全都是很可怕的數學。
* After finishing that class in Fall 2025, my whole perspective completely changed.
  > 上完這堂課之後，我的整個想法完全改觀了。
* Honestly, **if** I **hadn't taken** his lectures back then, I probably **wouldn't have built** that game at all. `【假設語氣 Type 3：與過去事實相反】`
  > 老實說，如果我當初沒有去聽他的課，現在大概根本做不出那款遊戲。
* **I've** kept rewatching his talks **for** over a year now—and **since** finishing the course, **I've already applied** so much of it to real projects. `【Unit 2.2 & 2.4 現在完成式：for / since / already】`
  > 我已經反覆回看他的演講超過一年了——而且從上完課到現在，我已經把許多觀念實際用到自己的專題上。

### 【Slide 4】
* Why do I like his teaching? He doesn't throw dry formulas at you.
  > 我為什麼喜歡他的教法？因為他不會直接丟一堆枯燥的公式給你。
* Instead, he turns the whole thing into three simple steps with funny cartoons:
  > 相反地，他用好笑的漫畫把整件事變成三個簡單步驟：
* first, decide what "good" means with a loss function;
  > 第一，用 Loss Function 決定什麼叫做「好」；
* next, pick a model;
  > 接著，挑一個模型；
* then, use gradient descent to slowly find the best answer.
  > 然後，用梯度下降法慢慢找出最佳解。
* Pretty simple, right?
  > 很直覺吧？

### 【Slide 5】
* Look at this loss curve from class.
  > 你看課堂上這張 Loss 曲線圖。
* **When** you train a model, **if** the line goes down like this, it means the computer is actually learning. `【Unit 4.4：when + if】`
  > 當你在訓練模型時，如果線像這樣往下降，代表電腦真的在學習。
* **If** the line gets stuck and stays flat, something is off—so you tweak your settings and try again. `【條件句：if】`
  > 如果線卡住不動變平了，就代表哪裡怪怪的——這時就微調設定再試一次。
* You'll definitely **make mistakes** at first—that's normal, you just **do your best** and fix them step by step. `【Unit 2.3 Word Friends：make mistakes / do your best】`
  > 一開始一定會出錯——這很正常，只要盡力做並一步步修正就好。

### 【Slide 6】
* He also connected these basics straight to modern Artificial Intelligence—like how Large Language Models learn from huge text, and how image models generate pictures.
  > 他也把這些基礎直接連到現代人工智慧——像是大型語言模型怎麼從海量文字學習，以及影像模型怎麼生圖。
* Plus, **he's put** every single lecture on YouTube for free. `【現在完成式：has put】`
  > 而且他把每一堂課都免費放在 YouTube 上。
* Even if **you've never studied** coding before, or **you've just started** learning, you can still pick it up anytime. `【Unit 2.2 現在完成式：never / just】`
  > 就算你以前從沒學過寫程式，或是才剛開始學，隨時都還是能上手。

### 【Slide 7】
* Before taking his course, I **had never had** the chance to train a real model myself. `【課綱：had had】`
  > 修他的課以前，我從來沒機會自己訓練一個真正的模型。
* My first few tries failed, and yeah, I wanted to quit.
  > 前幾次嘗試都失敗，老實講我那時超想放棄。
* But I didn't **give up**. `【Unit 4.3 片語動詞：give up】`
  > 但我撐下來沒有放棄。
* **If** you keep testing your code, you'll **end up** building cool stuff—like predicting game ratings or customizing AI images. `【Unit 4.4 First Conditional + Unit 4.3 end up】`
  > 只要你不斷測試程式，最後就能做出很酷的東西——像是預測遊戲評分，或客製化 AI 生成圖片。
* **If** I **had** more time today, I**'d show** you the whole demo live! `【假設語氣 Type 2：與現在事實相反】`
  > 如果今天時間更充裕的話，我就直接現場展示整個作品給大家看！

### 【Slide 8】
* Looking back after finishing the class, it still means a lot to me—clear, fun, and hands-on.
  > 上完這堂課到現在回頭看，它對我還是意義很大——清楚、有趣，又能動手實作。
* I'm actually applying what I learned to other projects right now—**I haven't finished** them all **yet**, but I'm going to keep building and sharing more. `【Unit 2.2 現在完成式：yet ＋ 進行中專題】`
  > 我目前也正在把所學用到其他 project 當中——雖然還沒全部做完，但我會繼續打造並分享更多作品。
* My last slide has all the references I used.
  > 最後一張投影片是我參考的資料來源。
* Thanks for listening!
  > 謝謝大家！
